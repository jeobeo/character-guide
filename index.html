The plan

Rig the stylized base body (BodyBaseMesh_01.fbx) fresh in AccuRIG. That skeleton becomes the one skeleton for everything. Bodies and clothes are skinned to it, masks and hats sit on a head socket, and Unity swaps pieces at runtime without touching animation or IK.

One skeleton

The new rig, Scrappy_Rig. Every body and clothing piece is skinned to it in the same A-pose. Once M1 ships, nobody renames or re-poses its bones.

One piece, one FBX

Skinned pieces export with the skeleton (SK_). Masks and hats export as plain meshes (SM_). New content never needs new code.

Hide skin, don't delete it

The body stays whole. Each item lists the body regions it covers and the shader hides them, so any outfit works on any body.

Decisions

What we're doing, and why it beats the alternatives for this game.

AreaDecisionWhy
RigFresh AccuRIG rig on the base body. Its A-pose is the rest pose.The old rig was fitted to the clothed mesh and cut down to 62 bones. The clothes were modelled on this A-pose body, and A-pose deforms shoulders better. Unity handles it with Enforce T-Pose.
BonesKeep AccuRIG's CC_Base_* names and the four arm twist bones per side. Remove helper, toe, breast and face bones.Twist bones stop wrists pinching when holding tools; FinalIK's TwistRelaxer drives them. Helper bones need drivers Unity doesn't have.
Rig fileThe skeleton exports on its own as SK_Scrappy_Rig.fbx and owns the avatar.Bodies can change or multiply without touching the avatar or the prefab.
ClothesSkinned to the same skeleton. Weights copied from the body, then cleaned.The standard approach for outfits in multiplayer games. Keeps Animancer, IK and hit reactions untouched.
Masks & hatsPlain meshes on a head socket. The team decides the mask.Masks are the main customisation and there will be many. No weights to paint, cheapest to draw, and usable on menus and banners.
SkinOne whole body. Covered regions collapse in the shader. Skin tone is a tint.One body works under every outfit; collapsing keeps it opaque and cheap.
SkinningGPU (Batched) mesh deformation.The project skins on the CPU today, which grows with every piece and player.
Not doingUMA-style frameworks, one mesh per outfit, deleting skin per outfit.They fight the existing rig and prefab, or multiply files with every mask and outfit.

Alex

1 · Rig the base body in AccuRIG

  1. Load BodyBaseMesh_01.fbx (no skeleton, A-pose, about 26k triangles). Place the markers carefully: wrists, elbows and shoulders decide how everything deforms.
  2. Rig with full five-finger hands. Test the pose previews for shoulder and elbow collapse, and adjust markers if needed.
  3. Export as FBX for Blender, mesh plus skeleton, in the original A-pose, no animation.
Alex

2 · Clean the rig in Blender

  1. Import the FBX into the master file with Automatic Bone Orientation on. Select the armature and body and apply all transforms (Ctrl A › All Transforms) so both sit at scale 1.
  2. Rename the armature object to Scrappy_Rig and the body to Body_M_01. Leave bone names as AccuRIG made them.
  3. Keep: the Humanoid chain (CC_Base_Hip, spine, neck, head, clavicles, arms, hands, fingers, legs, feet, ToeBase) and the arm twist bones (UpperarmTwist01/02, ForearmTwist01/02 on each side).
  4. Remove, moving each bone's weights into its parent first: all ShareBones, the individual toe bones, ThighTwist/CalfTwist, Breast, RibsTwist, and the face bones (jaw, teeth, tongue, eyes). To move weights: Vertex Weight Mix modifier on the body, group A = parent, group B = the bone, Add, All; apply, then delete the vertex group and the bone.
  5. The Armature modifier on the body points at Scrappy_Rig. The rig's current A-pose is its rest pose from now on.
Alex

3 · Body weights

  1. Weight Paint with X-Mirror on. Pose-test: arms up and forward, elbows and knees at 90°, spine twist, wrist roll, crouch. Fix shoulders, armpits, elbows, hips, knees and wrists.
  2. The wrist twist should spread along the forearm through ForearmTwist01/02, not collapse at the wrist.
  3. Finish with Weights › Smooth, Limit Total 4, Normalize All. Laptops on the Performant setting use only 2 bones per vertex, so check the shoulders still hold up.
Alex

Alex: clothes & masks

Alex

4 · Get the clothes onto the A-pose body

The clothes must sit on Body_M_01 in the A-pose. Measured from the files: the clothed Scrappy's head, torso and legs already match the base body, and only the arms differ, because the old rig put them in a T-pose.

  1. If you still have the clothes from before the old AccuRIG pass, which are already in the A-pose, use those and skip to 4.
  2. Otherwise import Assets/Meshes/CM_Prefab_01/CM_Scrappy.fbx. Pose its old Skeleton so its hands and sleeves sit on the base body's arms. Its hands are the base body's hands, so they should fit exactly.
  3. Apply the Armature modifier on the clothing mesh, then delete the old skeleton and the clothing's skin pieces (the Body_A material).
  4. Split into items: Edit Mode, P › By Material, then L and P › Selection. Name them with the naming pattern and put each in its slot collection.
Alex

5 · Weight every clothing piece from the body

  1. Delete the old vertex groups on each piece. Add an Armature modifier to Scrappy_Rig.
  2. Copy weights from Body_M_01 with the free Robust Weight Transfer add-on, which fills sensible weights where cloth sits away from the body. Blender's Data Transfer modifier (Vertex Groups, Nearest Face Interpolated) is the built-in fallback.
  3. Pose-test with the body visible. Fix intersections at elbows, knees and armpits by smoothing the cloth's weights toward the body's, not by moving the cloth.
  4. Rigid bits on clothing (buckles, plates): weight them to one bone so they don't bend.
  5. Limit Total 4, Normalize All.
Alex

10 · Export everything with one command

From the repository root. It exports the rig, every skinned piece (SK_) and every mask or hat (SM_) into the Unity project with identical settings. Names starting with _ are skipped.

export_scrappy_parts.py (save in ArtSource/Tools/) 
"""Exports the rig, every body and every wardrobe piece in Scrappy_Master.blend for Unity.

blender -b Scrappy_Master.blend --python export_scrappy_parts.py -- <unity project folder>
"""
import os
import sys
import bpy

ARMATURE = "Scrappy_Rig"                     # armature object name
OUT = "Assets/Art/Characters/Scrappy"
FOLDERS = {                                  # Blender collection -> Unity folder
    "Bodies": "Bodies",
    "Torso": "Wardrobe/Torso", "Legs": "Wardrobe/Legs", "Feet": "Wardrobe/Feet",
    "Hands": "Wardrobe/Hands", "Mask": "Wardrobe/Mask", "Headwear": "Wardrobe/Headwear",
    "Backpack": "Wardrobe/Backpack", "Accessories": "Wardrobe/Accessories",
}
SOCKET = {"Mask", "Headwear"}                # plain meshes on Socket_Head, exported in place
REGIONS = ["Head", "Face", "Neck", "Chest", "Stomach", "UpperArms",
           "Forearms", "Hands", "Hips", "Thighs", "Shins", "Feet"]

project = os.path.abspath(sys.argv[sys.argv.index("--") + 1])
armature = bpy.data.objects[ARMATURE]


def bake_regions(obj):
    """Bodies: Region_* vertex groups -> second UV map 'Regions' (u = region index, one value per face)."""
    index_of = {g.index: REGIONS.index(g.name[7:]) for g in obj.vertex_groups
                if g.name.startswith("Region_") and g.name[7:] in REGIONS}
    if not index_of:
        return
    mesh = obj.data
    uv = mesh.uv_layers.get("Regions") or mesh.uv_layers.new(name="Regions")
    region_of = {}
    for v in mesh.vertices:
        for g in v.groups:
            if g.group in index_of and g.weight > 0.5:
                region_of[v.index] = index_of[g.group]
    for poly in mesh.polygons:
        votes = {}
        for vi in poly.vertices:
            if vi in region_of:
                votes[region_of[vi]] = votes.get(region_of[vi], 0) + 1
        region = max(votes, key=votes.get) if votes else 0
        for li in poly.loop_indices:
            uv.data[li].uv = (region + 0.5, 0.0)


def export(objects, path):
    bpy.ops.object.select_all(action="DESELECT")
    for o in objects:
        o.hide_set(False)
        o.select_set(True)
    bpy.context.view_layer.objects.active = objects[0]
    os.makedirs(os.path.dirname(path), exist_ok=True)
    bpy.ops.export_scene.fbx(
        filepath=path, use_selection=True, object_types={"ARMATURE", "MESH"},
        apply_unit_scale=True, apply_scale_options="FBX_SCALE_UNITS",
        axis_forward="-Z", axis_up="Y",
        use_mesh_modifiers=True, mesh_smooth_type="FACE",
        add_leaf_bones=False, use_armature_deform_only=True,
        primary_bone_axis="Y", secondary_bone_axis="X",
        bake_anim=False, path_mode="STRIP", embed_textures=False)
    print("Exported", path)


export([armature], os.path.join(project, OUT, "Rig", "SK_Scrappy_Rig.fbx"))

for collection, folder in FOLDERS.items():
    coll = bpy.data.collections.get(collection)
    if coll is None:
        continue
    for obj in coll.all_objects:
        if obj.type != "MESH" or obj.name.startswith("_"):
            continue
        skinned = any(m.type == "ARMATURE" for m in obj.modifiers)
        if collection in SOCKET:
            if skinned:
                print("SKIPPED (masks and hats must not be skinned):", obj.name)
                continue
            export([obj], os.path.join(project, OUT, folder, "SM_" + obj.name + ".fbx"))
            continue
        if not skinned:
            print("SKIPPED (not skinned to the rig):", obj.name)
            continue
        if collection == "Bodies":
            bake_regions(obj)
        export([obj, armature], os.path.join(project, OUT, folder, "SK_" + obj.name + ".fbx"))

In Unity, press Ctrl R to pick the files up (auto-refresh is off in this project).

ScrappyCharacterImportRules.cs (editor assembly) 
using System.IO;
using System.Linq;
using UnityEditor;
using UnityEngine;

namespace Scrapsized.Editor
{
    /// <summary>
    /// First-import settings for Scrappy character content, chosen by folder. The rig owns the avatar; every skinned
    /// piece copies it (which also flags a renamed bone). Masks and hats are plain meshes.
    /// </summary>
    sealed class ScrappyCharacterImportRules : AssetPostprocessor
    {
        const string Root = "Assets/Art/Characters/Scrappy/";
        const string Rig = Root + "Rig/SK_Scrappy_Rig.fbx";

        void OnPreprocessModel()
        {
            if (!assetPath.StartsWith(Root)) return;
            var importer = (ModelImporter)assetImporter;
            if (!importer.importSettingsMissing) return; // only the first import; later tweaks stand

            string file = Path.GetFileName(assetPath);
            bool animation = assetPath.StartsWith(Root + "Animations/");

            importer.importAnimation = animation;
            importer.materialImportMode = ModelImporterMaterialImportMode.None; // materials live on the item asset
            importer.generateSecondaryUV = false;                               // UV1 carries body regions
            importer.isReadable = false;

            if (file.StartsWith("SM_"))
            {
                importer.animationType = ModelImporterAnimationType.None;       // masks and hats
                return;
            }

            importer.animationType = ModelImporterAnimationType.Human;
            importer.optimizeGameObjects = false;
            importer.skinWeights = ModelImporterSkinWeights.Standard;           // 4 bones per vertex

            if (assetPath == Rig || (animation && !file.StartsWith("A_Scrappy_")))
            {
                importer.avatarSetup = ModelImporterAvatarSetup.CreateFromThisModel; // the rig, or bought animations
            }
            else
            {
                importer.avatarSetup = ModelImporterAvatarSetup.CopyFromOther;
                importer.sourceAvatar = AssetDatabase.LoadAllAssetsAtPath(Rig).OfType<Avatar>().FirstOrDefault();
            }
        }
    }
}

4 · Wearing things

  • Masks and hats (SM_): instantiate under Socket_Head at zero. The mask is the team's (TeamMasks) while the character has one, otherwise the player's pick. BGMCharacter.OnTeamChanged, empty today, re-applies it.
  • Skinned pieces (SK_): instantiate under Mesh, rebind bones by name (below), delete the piece's own skeleton copy, set layer Character, assign materials, give any SpringChain the body colliders.
  • After any change: hidden regions = every worn item's Hides combined, written to the body.
  • Switching body: swap the Body piece. Each item then keeps its prefab if it fits, swaps to the new fit, or falls back to the slot default (the choice is remembered for switching back). No avatar change, no Rebind(), no IK reset.
  • Set skinnedMotionVectors = false on pieces. Add CharacterAppearanceView and SpringChain to BGMCharacter.ServerOnlyCosmeticComponentNames.
SkeletonBinder.cs (rebind by bone name) 
using System.Collections.Generic;
using UnityEngine;

/// <summary>
/// Moves a wardrobe piece onto the character's skeleton by bone name. Item bones (backpack flap, straps) are re-homed
/// under the character's bone that matches their original parent, keeping their local rest offset.
/// </summary>
public static class SkeletonBinder
{
    public static Dictionary<string, Transform> Index(Transform skeletonRoot)
    {
        var bones = new Dictionary<string, Transform>();
        foreach (var t in skeletonRoot.GetComponentsInChildren<Transform>(true)) bones[t.name] = t;
        return bones;
    }

    public static void Bind(SkinnedMeshRenderer piece, Dictionary<string, Transform> master)
    {
        Transform[] bones = piece.bones;
        for (int i = 0; i < bones.Length; i++)
        {
            Transform own = bones[i];
            if (own == null) continue;
            if (master.TryGetValue(own.name, out Transform shared)) { bones[i] = shared; continue; }

            // Item bone. Only the top of each chain moves; its children come with it.
            if (own.parent != null && master.TryGetValue(own.parent.name, out Transform newParent))
                own.SetParent(newParent, false);
        }
        piece.bones = bones;
        if (piece.rootBone != null && master.TryGetValue(piece.rootBone.name, out Transform root))
            piece.rootBone = root;
    }
}

5 · Body shader

SG_ScrappyBody: URP Lit Shader Graph, opaque, with BaseMap, Normal and MaskMap like URP Lit.

  • Skin tone: Color _SkinTone. Base Color = Lerp(BaseMap.rgb, BaseMap.rgb × _SkinTone, BaseMap.a).
  • Hidden regions (vertex stage): Float _HiddenRegions. UV1.x › Floor = region; Power(2, region) › Divide _HiddenRegions by it › Floor › Modulo 2 = hidden. Vertex Position = Position (Object) × (1 − hidden). Hidden faces shrink to a point, so nothing is drawn or shadowed and the body stays opaque.
  • Set both on every body material through renderer.materials (per-character copies; destroy them with the character).
  • SG_ScrappyMask: the same tint, fed with the team colour.

6 · Backpack springs

One SpringChain per item, bones listed parents first. Start with Backpack_Main stiffness 0.35, damping 0.3, and Backpack_Flap 0.08, 0.15. It stops beyond 30 m, is off on servers, and the weather can push it through SpringChain.Wind. For real cloth later (capes), look at Magica Cloth 2 rather than Unity Cloth.

SpringChain.cs, SpringCollider.cs 
using UnityEngine;

/// <summary>
/// Verlet secondary motion for an item's spring bones (Backpack_Main, Backpack_Flap, strap links). One component
/// per item; bones are listed parents before children. Each bone starts from its rest pose under its already-solved
/// parent every frame, so animation stays in charge and this only adds lag, sway and bounce.
/// </summary>
[DefaultExecutionOrder(20000)] // after Animancer, FinalIK and socket followers (10000)
public sealed class SpringChain : MonoBehaviour
{
    [System.Serializable]
    public struct Bone
    {
        public Transform Transform;
        [Range(0f, 1f)] public float Stiffness;      // pull back towards the animated pose
        [Range(0f, 1f)] public float Damping;
    }

    public Bone[] Bones;                             // parents before children; _Tip bones are not listed
    public Vector3 Gravity = new Vector3(0f, -1.5f, 0f);
    public float Radius = 0.04f;
    public float MaxDistance = 30f;
    public SpringCollider[] Colliders;               // body proxies, set on equip
    public static Vector3 Wind;                      // set by the weather system

    Quaternion[] _rest;
    Vector3[] _localTip, _tip, _previous;

    void OnEnable()
    {
        int n = Bones.Length;
        _rest = new Quaternion[n]; _localTip = new Vector3[n]; _tip = new Vector3[n]; _previous = new Vector3[n];
        for (int i = 0; i < n; i++)
        {
            Transform bone = Bones[i].Transform;
            _rest[i] = bone.localRotation;
            _localTip[i] = TipOf(bone);
            _tip[i] = _previous[i] = bone.TransformPoint(_localTip[i]);
        }
    }

    // The bone's "_Tip" child gives the lever; otherwise its first child.
    static Vector3 TipOf(Transform bone)
    {
        foreach (Transform child in bone) if (child.name.EndsWith("_Tip")) return child.localPosition;
        return bone.childCount > 0 ? bone.GetChild(0).localPosition : Vector3.up * 0.1f;
    }

    void LateUpdate()
    {
        Camera view = Camera.main;
        bool near = view == null || (view.transform.position - transform.position).sqrMagnitude < MaxDistance * MaxDistance;
        float dt = Mathf.Min(Time.deltaTime, 1f / 30f);
        for (int i = 0; i < Bones.Length; i++)
        {
            Transform bone = Bones[i].Transform;
            bone.localRotation = _rest[i];                   // rest pose under the parent solved this frame
            Vector3 target = bone.TransformPoint(_localTip[i]);
            if (!near) { _tip[i] = _previous[i] = target; continue; }

            Vector3 next = _tip[i] + (_tip[i] - _previous[i]) * (1f - Bones[i].Damping) + (Gravity + Wind) * (dt * dt);
            next += (target - next) * Bones[i].Stiffness;
            foreach (SpringCollider c in Colliders) if (c != null) next = c.PushOut(next, Radius);
            float length = (target - bone.position).magnitude;
            next = bone.position + (next - bone.position).normalized * length;

            _previous[i] = _tip[i];
            _tip[i] = next;
            bone.rotation = Quaternion.FromToRotation(target - bone.position, next - bone.position) * bone.rotation;
        }
    }
}

/// <summary>A body proxy for springs. Not a physics collider: the character controller and raycasts never see it.</summary>
public sealed class SpringCollider : MonoBehaviour
{
    public float Radius = 0.12f;

    public Vector3 PushOut(Vector3 point, float extra)
    {
        Vector3 offset = point - transform.position;
        float r = Radius + extra;
        return offset.sqrMagnitude < r * r ? transform.position + offset.normalized * r : point;
    }
}

Adding a bought clip

  1. Drop it in Assets/Art/Characters/Scrappy/Animations/; it imports as Humanoid.
  2. Rig › Configure: every bone green.
  3. Loop Time for cycles. Bake root rotation, Y and XZ into the pose (the character doesn't use root motion), except clips named [RM].
  4. Assign it to the right Animancer state on CharacterVisual. Upper-body clips use GunUpperMask.
Alex

Animating in Blender

  1. Animate Scrappy_Rig, one Action per clip. Don't key swing bones.
  2. Export the armature only as A_Scrappy_<Name>.fbx (_Loop for cycles), same settings as the script with Bake Animation on.
  3. Save into the Animations/ folder. It copies the rig's avatar, so it matches exactly.

Don't do this

  • Change a bone in Scrappy_Rig after M1Renaming, reparenting, re-rolling or re-posing breaks the prefab's IK and sockets, the avatar and every item. Adding new swing bones is fine.
  • Move or rename FBX files in ExplorerUnity loses their IDs and everything pointing at them. Move them inside Unity only.
  • Export by handDifferent settings make pieces float or twist. Always use the script.
  • Weight a mask or hatThey're plain meshes on the socket; the export skips skinned ones.
  • Reuse or change an item or body IDSaved outfits and other players show the wrong thing. Retire old IDs instead.
  • Stack up piecesMore than about 7 renderers per character gets expensive with 16 players on laptops.
